Novelist Quickstart Guide

Welcome to Novelist, the ultimate app for planning, writing, and organizing your stories. This guide will walk you through the basics so you can confidently start your storytelling journey.

1. Library: Your Collection of Projects

When you open the app, you’ll find your Library—a central hub for managing all your writing universes.

  • Create a project: Tap the “+” button. Choose the Novel standard template or a custom template based on an existing project. A project acts as the master container for your story's universe.
  • Edit, set cover or delete project: Press the three dots menu for options.
  • Unlimited projects: There’s no limit to how many projects you can create!
  • Project safety indicators: Projects with a green indicator are safe, while those with a red indicator need to be backed up to avoid losing your work.

Tap a project to access its main workspace.

2. The Project Workspace: Shared Elements

Inside a project, you'll build the foundation of your universe. The Plot and Write sections serve as a shared pool of resources for all the books you will create within this project.

2.1 Plot: Plan Your Universe’s Building Blocks

This is where you create and manage the core elements of your world: Themes, Characters, Events, and more.

  • Add items: Tap the “+” button to create a new item. Each item needs a title (required) and optionally a synopsis.
  • Customize categories: Create, edit, or delete categories to suit your writing style (e.g., add “Factions” or “Planets”).
  • Organize items: Use the status circle to track each item’s progress. Long press the circle to delete or merge items. Long press an item to reorder them.
  • Detailed item view: Tap an item to edit its details, relationships, images, and rich text descriptions.

2.2 Write: Draft Your Scenes

Scenes are the backbone of your writing. In this section, you draft them into a shared pool, ready to be used in any book within the project.

  • Create scenes: Tap the “+” button to add a new scene with a title (required).
  • Rich text editor: Write your story using formatting options. Mention Plot items using the “@” symbol to link your universe together.
  • Manage scenes: Attach Plot items, split scenes into two, or rearrange them in the list by long pressing.
👉 Important: Scenes are drafted here, but they won't appear in a final manuscript until you attach them to a specific Book (see next section).

3. Books: Structuring Your Manuscripts

A single project can contain one or multiple Books. Because they live inside the same project, all books share the exact same Plot items and Scenes—perfect for writing a series, sequels, or spin-offs!

3.1 Organize: Structure Your Book

Once you enter a Book, the Organize section helps you shape it into a coherent manuscript, dividing it into acts, parts, and chapters.

  • Add sections: Create a tree-like structure to break your book into chapters or other divisions.
  • Attach scenes: Choose scenes from the project's shared Write pool that belong in this specific book.
  • Rearrange sections: Easily reorder chapters to adjust your book’s flow.
👉 Important: Only scenes attached to a book's Organize section are included in its final manuscript.

4. Schedule: Set Goals and Track Progress

Stay motivated with clear goals and track your writing progress over time.

  • Set goals: Choose a word count target, a deadline, or both.
  • Daily progress: See a chart of your word count each day to keep yourself accountable.

5. Preview and Export Your Book

When your book is structured and ready:

  • Preview: Tap the eye icon inside a book to see how your manuscript will look.
  • Export your book: Tap Export and share to create your final manuscript in EPUB, ODT, or HTML format.

6. Backup and Sync: Safeguard Your Work

  • Backups: Save your project data locally or to the cloud (Google Drive or iCloud).
  • Manual sync: Use Send to cloud and Receive from cloud to manually transfer content between devices.
👉 Important: Make frequent backups to avoid losing your work due to device issues.

7. Tips for New Users

  • Start small: Create a single project with one book to get comfortable with the workflow.
  • Embrace the shared universe: Don't be afraid to create character profiles in the Plot section before you start writing your Scenes.
  • Backup often: Don’t risk losing your hard work.

You’re now ready to start your writing adventure with Novelist! Take your time to explore each feature, and remember: your creativity is the only limit. Happy writing! ✍️
